Assist Companion. Specs marked ✓ Assist Companion are finished in the addon: Frost, Unholy and Blood Death Knight, Devastation and Augmentation Evoker, Windwalker Monk. Their highlight DPS and losses include the addon's corrections. Each figure is the page's highlight DPS scaled by what the addon's rules gained over SimC's highlight in that spec's rules sim (same hero tree as the build here), so treat them as estimates. Hover a loss for the highlight alone. Augmentation isn't simmed (its value is mostly allies' damage), so it shows the highlight alone. In testing specs are being tested in game and still show the highlight alone. Every other spec has no addon support yet and shows the highlight alone.

Talent builds. Every spec uses the build a player on the optimal rotation would use: SimC's default build for single target, Noxxic's top AoE build for 5-target cleave, and the best of every candidate build with the optimal rotation for 3-target cleave. Assist Companion is tested on these builds.

Optimal rotation DPS is the optimal rotation on its own best build. DPS lost compares the highlight (with Assist Companion where marked) against it.

Rank orders specs by combined loss, the plain average of the single-target, 3-target and 5-target losses, for the selected builds. Rank 1 loses the least.

Fights. Single target is one raid boss; 3-target cleave is a boss plus two adds; 5-target cleave is a boss plus four adds. Assisted Highlight is SimC's use_blizzard_action_list=1, which plays the in-game highlight's priority from game data, plus a basic cooldown list SimC adds. Both rotations are played perfectly.

Talent strings are standard in-game exports. In game, open Talents, click the loadout dropdown, choose Import, and paste.

Re-simmed specs. SimC's highlight sim can't press a spell's replacement while a button is overridden (Chaos Strike during Metamorphosis becomes Annihilation, Raging Blow becomes Crushing Blow, and so on), so it understated the highlight for Devourer and Havoc Demon Hunter, Shadow Priest, Retribution, Affliction, Fury, Marksmanship, Survival and Guardian. Those specs (tagged Re-simmed) were re-simmed with every override pair from game data added after its base spell. Their losses apply the re-sim's correction per fight (the optimal rotation's builds weren't re-simmed), so treat them as estimates. Highlight DPS for these specs is derived from the corrected loss. Hover a loss to see SimC's original figure. The biggest changes: Devourer −55.7% → −29.6% combined, Havoc −34.6% → −12.3%, Shadow −27.3% → −10.0%.

Not counted here: cooldowns the in-game highlight never shows (SimC presses them from its basic cooldown list), so real in-game losses are larger for specs whose big cooldowns are missing from the highlight, e.g. Frost Death Knight: about −1.6% here, about −20% following only the in-game highlight. Devastation: about −31% here, about −53% following only the in-game highlight (no Dragonrage, Deep Breath or Tip the Scales).

Devastation builds re-checked (Nov 6): every build on the page was the starting point for a search over every choice-node swap at each target count. The optimal rotation's builds are unchanged. The highlight's best build swaps Event Horizon for Eye of Infinity at all three target counts (+1.8% / +0.1% / +0.6% at 1 / 3 / 5 targets). All six best builds are Scalecommander. Swaps that move points between nodes aren't covered, because SimC's data has no tree connections.

† Balance, Guardian and Devastation only have last season's gear profile in SimC, so their raw DPS reads low. Augmentation (added Nov 6) has no SimC profile: it uses Devastation's gear, and its DPS is everything it adds to a raid, meaning its own damage plus its buffs on the 8 stand-in allies SimC gives a lone Augmentation (raid DPS minus the same raid with an idle Augmentation). Its builds are the best of Icy Veins' four (Chronowarden and Scalecommander, single target and AoE) after a choice-node search. Their loss percentages are still valid. Tank losses count damage only; the highlight never suggests defensives.
